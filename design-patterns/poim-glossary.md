**Glossary of the Patterns of Information Management**

Appendix 1 of *Patterns of Information Management* is a three-column table of Topic,
Description and Related Patterns.  Each topic becomes a glossary term; the Related Patterns
column becomes a `Link Semantic Assignment` onto the design pattern created by the chapter
files in this directory.

## Create Glossary Term
> Creates or updates a glossary term — a concept, phrase, or word defined within a glossary.

### Display Name

Activity

### Glossary Name

- Glossary::PatternsOfInformationManagement

### Qualified Name

GlossaryTerm::PatternsOfInformationManagement::Activity

### Summary

A piece of work that is being performed by a person, an IT system, or both. Many activities within an organization are well defined— someone has defined how that type of work will be performed. Well-defined activities have well-defined information needs and are often cost effective to support with IT systems. However, there are always unexpected events and circumstances that need to be handled by people through ad hoc activities.
