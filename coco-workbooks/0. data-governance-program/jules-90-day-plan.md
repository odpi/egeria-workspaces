This plan sets out how Jules Keeper approached her first ninety days as Chief Data Officer, structured as three sequential thirty-day phases: learning the organisation, initiating strategic work, and demonstrating impact.

## Create Project

### Display Name
First 30 Days (Learning Phase)

### Qualified Name
JulesKeeper::Project::First30DaysLearningPhase

### Project Phase
Learning

### Mission
To understand the team, the organisation, and its data landscape before proposing any changes.

### Description
First things first, I needed to get to know my team, understand their skills, current role, ambitions, and opinions on what should change. These were my primary allies in my new role. Then I needed to do the rounds with key people and teams across the organization. This was the best way to learn about the company's operations, products, services, and workflows, and it was also my starting point for building relationships across the business. I made sure to ask how they use data today and what they could do if they had additional data. Finally I needed to document the evidence I uncovered and outline my approach to share with the board.

### Success Criteria
- Understood the team's skills, current roles, ambitions, and opinions on what should change
- Met with key people and teams across the organization to learn about operations, products, services, and workflows
- Asked how each team uses data today and what they could do with additional data
- Documented the evidence uncovered and outlined an approach to share with the board

### Sub-Projects
- JulesKeeper::Task::First30DaysLearningPhase::MeetMyTeam
- JulesKeeper::Task::First30DaysLearningPhase::MeetLeadersAcrossBusiness
- JulesKeeper::Task::First30DaysLearningPhase::BuildBoardProposal
